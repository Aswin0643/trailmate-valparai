BEFORE ENTERING VALPARAI

Apply for your Valparai e-pass

The Tamil Nadu e-pass portal is the official place to register your vehicle and trip. Complete the application before departure and save the QR/pass on your phone.

Open official TN e-Pass portal ↗

Keep the approved pass available offline. Check the portal's current requirements for your travel date, vehicle and passengers. Do not assume a pass guarantees access through the Malakkappara–Vazhachal forest road; confirm two-wheeler permissions and checkpoint timings with authorities.
